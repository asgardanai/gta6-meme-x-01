An entry for [make.fun's GTA 6 meme bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01).

The meme: a tiny PS5 next to a gigantic glowing GTA VI disc — the game is
250 GB and the console has 12 GB left. The make.fun ghost sits on the shelf
beside the console, holding its magenta star coin.

`POST.json` names the X post carrying the meme; `main.ts` copies it to
`/output/POST.json` for the gate.
